CROWDFUNDING - PRESENTATION TIPS
Before your presentation, check the following points and remember to
include them in your speech:
About the section ‘Your idea & goal’:
- Clear, compelling description of what the project is
- Why it matters / what problem solves
- Who is behind the project
- What makes your project special or different
- Why supporters should choose you over similar campaigns
About the section ‘Your audience’:
- Who will care about this project?
- How will you reach them? (social channels, communities, press…)
About the section ‘Potential press connections, networks
- A campaign video
- Photos, prototypes, mockups, demos
- Story that connects emotionally
- Testimonials, expert endorsements, early adopters
- Founders’ backgrounds and successes
- Media or influencers mentions (if available)
- Pre-launch hype (email list, sneak peeks)
- Outreach plan
About the section ‘Legal matters (Type of crowdfunding)’:
- Type of crowdfunding chosen (reward, equity, donation and
- Reasons why that type of crowdfunding has been chosen
About the section ‘Timeline’:
- Key milestones (past + future)
- Production and delivery schedule that feels realistic
About the section ‘Budget ’:
- Total amount needed
- Transparent breakdown: production, marketing, fees, shipping, etc.
- Stretch goals if applicable
About the section ‘Perks for your backers’:
- Reward tiers with clear value
- Limited or exclusively perks to drive excitement
