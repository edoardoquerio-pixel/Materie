3) LOOK UP THE TOPIC. Here is a short list of questions that may help 
your investigation (feel free to use it or not): 

1. Purpose and Origin 
● What was the intended purpose of this cloud system? 
● Who were the target users or organizations? 
● What problem or need was it supposed to solve? 

2. Design and Implementation 
● How was the system designed (architecture, features, storage 

type)? 

● Were there technical limitations or design flaws that contributed to 

failure? 

● Did it have adequate security, reliability, and scalability from the 

start? 

3. Performance and User Experience 
● Did the system suffer from frequent downtime, slow performance, 

or sync errors? 

● Were users satisfied with the interface and usability? 
● How did customers respond to bugs or crashes? 
4. Market and Competition 
● How did it compare to competitors at the time? 
● Were there better alternatives available that overshadowed it? 
● Did pricing or business strategy play a role in its failure? 
5. Consequences and Lessons Learned 
● What were the main reasons the cloud system failed or lost 

popularity? 

● Were there security breaches or data losses? 
● Did the company try to fix or improve the system? Was it 

successful? 

● What can current and future cloud service developers learn from 

this failure?
