TYPES  OF  CROWDFUNDING  to  better  understand  
1)  Donation-Based  Crowdfunding:   
●  No  financial  return  for  backers  
 ●  Supporters  contribute  because  they  believe  in  the  cause  
 ●  Typically  used  for  charity ,  community  projects ,  social  campaigns ,  or  
humanitarian
 
initiatives
 
 ●  Motivations:  altruism,  solidarity,  emotional  connection  
 
Examples:  fundraising  for  disaster  relief,  medical  expenses,  social-impact  initiatives  
 
2)  Reward-Based  Crowdfunding:   
●  Backers  receive  non-financial  rewards  in  exchange  for  their  contribution  
 ●  Rewards  often  tied  to  the  product  or  service  being  developed  
 ●  Very  common  for  creative ,  tech ,  and  product  launch  campaigns  
 ●  Motivation:  early  access,  exclusive  perks,  being  part  of  innovation  
 


Examples:  gadgets,  apps,  games,  artistic  works  launched  on  Kickstarter  or  
Indiegogo
 
 
3)  Equity-Based  Crowdfunding:   
●  Backers  become  investors  and  receive  ownership  shares  (equity)  
 ●  Suitable  for  startups  and  scalable  businesses* ,  including  ICT  and  tech  
solutions
 
 ●  Regulated  by  financial  authorities  in  most  countries  
 ●  Motivation:  potential  financial  return  if  the  business  grows  or  gets  acquired  
 
Examples:  tech  startup  seeking  capital  to  scale  operations  
 
*  Scalable  businesses  are  businesses  that  can  grow  its  revenue  faster  than  its

4)
 
Lending-Based  Crowdfunding  (Crowdlending):  
●  Backers  contribute  money  as  a  loan  
 ●  The  project  creator  promises  to  repay  with  interest  
 ●  Often  used  for  SMEs  (Small  and  medium  enterprises) ,  real  estate,  or  
companies

 ●  Motivation:  predictable  return*  without  ownership
