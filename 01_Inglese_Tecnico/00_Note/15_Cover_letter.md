General  Cover  Letter  Scheme   
1.  Who  are  you?  
Present  yourself  briefly  including:   
●  current  status/profession  ●  years  of  experience  (if  relevant)  ●  professional  identity  
2.  What  defines  you?  
Highlight  3–4  personal/professional  strengths  such  as  
●  technical  skills  ●  soft  skills  ●  work  style  ●  mindset  
3.  What  have  you  done?  
Summarize  relevant  experiences  such  as:  
●  studies  ●  internships  ●  projects  ●  volunteer  work  


●  achievements  
4.  What  drives  you?  
Speak  about  your  personality  and  professional  attitude.  
5.  What  are  you  looking  for?  
Explain  what  type  of  opportunity  you  want.
